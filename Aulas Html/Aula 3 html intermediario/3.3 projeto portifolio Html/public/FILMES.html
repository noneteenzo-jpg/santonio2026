The Best Movies Acording to Enzo

My top 3 movies of ALL-TIME

YOUR NAME

I love this movie. The art direction is impeccable, the story is captivating, and the characters are incredible and charismatic.

AKIRA

*Akira* was one of the first anime films to be shown on television, and even today—in 2026—it remains a visual spectacle; the story is flawless, Neo-Tokyo is captivating, and the film continues to be referenced to this day.

TROPA DE ELITE

*TROPA DE ELITE* is a Brazilian film that portrays the life of police Captain Nascimento, following his work and his operations in the favelas; it is an incredible film with a captivating story.
